ReelDrop turns one idea into a finished faceless video. Drop in a topic or paste a script, and it writes the story, creates the visuals, records the voiceover, adds captions and music, and gives you a Short or Reel that's ready to post.

There's nothing to learn. No timeline, no editing software, and no juggling separate subscriptions for scripts, images, voice and video. You tell ReelDrop what the video is about, pick a voice and a language, and it does the rest.

It's built for creators running faceless channels in India, on mythology, history, motivation, facts and money, with narration in Hindi, English and Hinglish, and formats made for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. One person can run three channels without hiring an editor.

Your first three videos each month are free. Creator is $7 a month for 15 videos, and Studio is $23 a month for creators running several channels.
